# Wallet Manager version history

## V1 — Core transaction tracking

- Added local accounts, opening balances, and income, expense, and transfer records.
- Added date-grouped records and today's income, expense, and net totals.

## V2 — Categories and CSV export

- Added custom category management, icons, colors, and a searchable category picker.
- Added CSV export and blue styling for transfers.

## V3 — Analysis, search, and account history

- Added Records search, monthly and date-range analysis, a daily flow graph, and category breakdowns.
- Added account transaction history and analysis account selection.

## V4 — Backup, dark mode, and icons

- Added full backup and restore, dark mode, and a Settings screen.
- Added account selection for CSV export and expanded app and category icons.

## V4.1 — Analysis views

- Added a category expense wheel and a selector for calendar, category, and flow graph views.

## V4.2 — Visual refinements and account icons

- Added selectable account icons and refined selection controls, search, dropdowns, and dark-mode contrast.

## V4.3 — Records and analysis refinements

- Added a Today/month Records summary toggle and refined analysis views, chart interaction, and compact amounts.
- Improved layouts, headings, buttons, and category selection presentation.

## V4.3.1 — Visual refinements

- Refined Analysis summaries, calendar and Records styling, category rows, and Add, Save, and Delete actions.

## V4.3.2 — Button and color refinements

- Corrected action-button outlines and refined category colors and dark-mode transfer styling.

## V4.4 — Stability and design consistency

- Fixed reported Add record and Categories crashes and improved search dismissal.

## V5 — Stability and accessibility fixes
